
Compatibility with through-port interfaces:
-------------------------------------------

* In general, it is NOT RECOMMENDED that the SMART Card is used with other
  interfaces (especially the RAM Turbo when a cartridge is inserted in it.)
  The general rule is that if an expansion device has a ROM then it will
  *not work* with the SMART Card (or any other device that also has a ROM).


Users report that the SMART Card (v2) *does work* with the following
peripherals:


* Spectra SCART interface (disable the ROM and RS323 socket functionalities on the Spectra: dip switches 2-4:off)
  http://www.fruitcake.plus.com/Sinclair/Spectrum/Spectra/SpectraInterface.htm 

* ZX-HD from Byte Delight.
  https://www.bytedelight.com/?page_id=1800

* PC keyboard interface:
  http://www.benophetinternet.nl/hobby/pcki.htm
